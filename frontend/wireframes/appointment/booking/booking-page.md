# Appointment Booking Wireframe

## Page Objective
Allow patients to reserve a medical appointment.

## Multi-Step Form

### Step 1 — Specialty
- Select medical specialty

### Step 2 — Doctor
- Choose available doctor

### Step 3 — Date & Time
- Calendar selection
- Time slot selection

### Step 4 — Confirmation
- Appointment summary
- Validation button

## Additional Fields
- Consultation reason
- Notes

## Buttons
- Next
- Previous
- Confirm Appointment

## Expected Actions
- Reserve appointment
- Select availability
- Confirm booking
